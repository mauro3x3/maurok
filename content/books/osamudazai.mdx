I watched Aoi Bungaku, which adapted Osamu Dazai’s No Longer Human, and immediately knew I had to read the book myself.

It is such a masterpiece, and there’s a good reason why Dazai is considered one of Japan’s greatest authors.

It raises legitimate questions about our purpose, relationships, commitments, and obligations to other people and society as a whole.

Who are you? An individual? Or someone who is part of a broader collective society?
