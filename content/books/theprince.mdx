The Prince is honestly one of the easiest books I have read. Machiavelli is quite direct and straight to the point. In a sense it feels like reading multiple Twitter posts combined into one book. Not fully done yet, but thus far I am a big fan! I want to finish the book ASAP, so I can move on to Discourses on Livy (Which is his actual master piece!).
