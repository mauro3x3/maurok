I watched Aoi Bungaku that animated the story of No Longer Human by Osamu Dazai and I immediately knew I had to read the book myself. It is such a master piece and there's a good reason why Osamu is among the best authors of Japanese. It raises legitimate questions about our purpose, ties, commitment and obligations to other people and society as a whole. Who are you? An individual? Or someone part of the broader collective society?
